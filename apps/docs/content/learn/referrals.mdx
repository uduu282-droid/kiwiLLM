The Referrals page lets your organization share a referral link and earn credits when referred users become active customers.

<ThemedImage alt="Referrals" basePath="/learn/referrals" />

## Before the program unlocks

If your organization has not yet met the eligibility threshold, the page shows your progress toward unlocking referrals.

That usually includes:

- a progress indicator
- the remaining amount needed
- the referral earning explanation

## Once unlocked

The page typically shows:

### Your referral link

A shareable link tied to your organization.

### Referral stats

You can track things like:

- total referred users
- total referral earnings

## How it works

1. share your referral link
2. a new user signs up through that link
3. your organization earns credits based on the referral program rules

## Good uses

- invite teammates or peers who are evaluating LLM infrastructure
- share KiwiLLM with customers or developer communities
- track referral performance from one place instead of manually reconciling credits
