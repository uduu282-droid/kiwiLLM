The Billing page is your central hub for managing plans, payment methods, and hosted KiwiLLM billing settings.

<ThemedImage alt="Billing" basePath="/learn/billing" />

## Billing overview

The billing area can show your current hosted billing state, recent payment activity, and any plan-specific limits that apply to your organization.

## Plan Management

View and manage your subscription:

- See your current plan (Free, Pro, or Enterprise)
- Billing cycle information
- Click **Manage Subscription** to upgrade, downgrade, or cancel

## Payment Methods

Manage your saved payment methods:

- Add a new credit card or payment method
- View existing payment methods
- Update billing information

## Usage and spend controls

Depending on your plan, billing can also include controls such as:

- current spend visibility
- payment and invoice history
- hosted usage settings tied to your organization

For free-tier traffic, rate limits still apply even if billing is configured elsewhere in the product. See [Rate Limits](/resources/rate-limits) for the current hosted limits.
